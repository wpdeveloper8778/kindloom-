About PawLuxe

PawLuxe was born from a simple belief: our pets deserve the very best. Founded in Dubai, we are a premium pet care brand dedicated to curating the highest quality products for dogs, cats, birds, and small pets across the United Arab Emirates.

We understand that your pet is family. That's why every product on our shelves is carefully selected for its quality, safety, and nutritional value. From grain-free foods to orthopedic beds, interactive toys to eco-friendly accessories — we bring the world's best pet products to your doorstep.
